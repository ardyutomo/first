Usia 3–5 tahun · Ages 3–5

Ojan si Kancil dan Sepeda KecilOjan the Mouse-Deer and the Little Bike

Fabel tentang jatuh, mencoba lagi, dan menjadi teman yang baik. A fable about falling, trying again, and being a good friend.

Kenalan dengan tokohnyaMeet the characters

Ojan si Kancil

Kancil kecil yang sedang belajar naik sepeda. Helm merahnya selalu dipakai.

A little mouse-deer learning to ride a bike. He always wears his red helmet.

Vivi si Monyet

Monyet lincah yang suka tertawa. Ada pita kuning di kepalanya.

A lively monkey who loves to laugh. She wears a yellow bow.

Bu Guru Gajah · Teacher Elephant

Guru yang sabar dan baik hati. Belalainya bisa menyiramkan air.

A patient, kind teacher. Her trunk can pour water.

1Halaman satuPage one

Di tepi hutan, tinggal seekor kancil kecil bernama Ojan. Hari ini Ojan belajar naik sepeda roda dua. Kakinya mengayuh. Kayuh, kayuh. Sepedanya bergoyang-goyang.

At the edge of the forest lived a little mouse-deer named Ojan. Today, Ojan was learning to ride a two-wheeled bike. His feet pushed the pedals. Push, push. The bike wobbled and wobbled.

2Halaman duaPage two

Brak! Ojan jatuh. Lututnya perih. Vivi si Monyet melihat dari dahan pohon. Vivi tertawa keras. “Hihihi! Ojan jatuh!”

Crash! Ojan fell down. His knee stung. Vivi the Monkey was watching from a tree branch. Vivi laughed out loud. “Hee-hee-hee! Ojan fell!”

3Halaman tigaPage three

“Jangan ketawa,” kata Ojan. Suaranya bergetar. “Lututku sakit.”

“Please don’t laugh,” said Ojan. His voice was shaky. “My knee hurts.”

4Halaman empatPage four

Vivi berhenti tertawa. Ia turun dari pohon dan melihat lutut Ojan yang merah. “Maaf, Ojan,” kata Vivi pelan. “Aku kira kamu jatuhnya lucu.”

Vivi stopped laughing. She climbed down from the tree and saw Ojan’s red knee. “I’m sorry, Ojan,” Vivi said softly. “I thought your fall looked funny.”

5Halaman limaPage five

Bu Guru Gajah datang membawa ember. Dengan belalainya, Bu Guru membersihkan luka Ojan dengan air. Byur, pelan-pelan. “Jatuh itu bagian dari belajar,” kata Bu Guru. “Mau coba lagi hari ini, atau besok?”

Teacher Elephant came with a bucket. With her trunk, she cleaned Ojan’s cut with water. Splish, gently. “Falling is part of learning,” said Teacher Elephant. “Do you want to try again today, or tomorrow?”

6Halaman enamPage six

Ojan berpikir. Ia melihat sepedanya. Ia melihat jalan setapak. “Sekarang,” kata Ojan. “Tapi pelan-pelan.”

Ojan thought about it. He looked at his bike. He looked at the path. “Now,” said Ojan. “But slowly.”

7Halaman tujuhPage seven

Vivi berdiri di ujung jalan. “Ayo, Ojan! Ke sini!” Ojan mengayuh. Satu, dua, tiga kayuhan. Belum jatuh!

Vivi stood at the end of the path. “Come on, Ojan! Over here!” Ojan pedaled. One, two, three pushes. Still riding!

8Halaman delapanPage eight

Di ujung jalan, Vivi bertepuk tangan. Prok, prok, prok! Kali ini Vivi tidak tertawa. Kali ini Vivi bersorak. “Hore! Ojan bisa!”

At the end of the path, Vivi clapped her hands. Clap, clap, clap! This time, Vivi did not laugh. This time, Vivi cheered. “Hooray! Ojan did it!”

Pesan moral · Moral of the story

Kalau teman jatuh, jangan ditertawakan. Bantu dan semangati dia.

When a friend falls, don’t laugh. Help them and cheer them on.

Nilai yang termuat dalam ceritaValues in this story

1. Perbuatan terpuji atau baik · Good deeds

Menyemangati teman yang sedang belajar, bukan menertawakannya.

Cheering on a friend who is learning, instead of laughing at them.

2. Perbuatan tidak terpuji atau jangan ditiru · Don’t copy this

Menertawakan teman yang jatuh atau sedang kesulitan.

Laughing at a friend who falls or is having a hard time.

3. Hak Asasi Manusia · Human rights

Hak setiap orang untuk diperlakukan dengan hormat dan tidak direndahkan.

Everyone has the right to be treated with respect and not be put down.

4. Ajak Bicara (Orang tua/keluarga/Guru) · Let’s talk (parents, family, teachers)

Apa yang bisa kamu katakan kalau temanmu jatuh?

What can you say when your friend falls?

“Kamu tidak apa-apa?”“Are you okay?” “Ayo, aku bantu.”“Come on, I’ll help you.” “Coba lagi, kamu pasti bisa!”“Try again, you can do it!”
