Usia 3–5 tahun · Ages 3–5

Ojan dan Sepeda KecilOjan and the Little Bike

Cerita bergambar tentang jatuh, mencoba lagi, dan menjadi teman yang baik. A picture story about falling, trying again, and being a good friend.

Kenalan dengan tokohnyaMeet the characters

Ojan

Anak yang sedang belajar naik sepeda. Helm birunya selalu dipakai.

A boy learning to ride a bike. He always wears his blue helmet.

Vivi

Anak ceria bertopi kuning. Suka tertawa, dan belajar untuk peduli.

A cheerful girl in a yellow hat. She loves to laugh, and learns to care.

Dimas

Teman yang sigap. Kalau ada yang jatuh, ia cepat mencari bantuan.

A quick-thinking friend. When someone falls, he runs for help.

Bu Guru · The teacher

Guru yang sabar dan selalu menenangkan.

A patient teacher who always helps everyone feel calm.

1Halaman satuPage one

Ojan belajar naik sepeda roda dua. Kakinya mengayuh. Kayuh, kayuh. Sepedanya bergoyang-goyang. Vivi dan Dimas menonton dari pinggir taman.

Ojan was learning to ride a two-wheeled bike. His feet pushed the pedals. Push, push. The bike wobbled and wobbled. Vivi and Dimas watched from the edge of the park.

2Halaman duaPage two

Brak! Ojan jatuh. Lututnya perih. Vivi yang melihat tertawa keras. “Hihihi! Ojan jatuh!” Dimas kaget.

Crash! Ojan fell down. His knee stung. Vivi saw it and laughed out loud. “Hee-hee-hee! Ojan fell!” Dimas was startled.

3Halaman tigaPage three

“Jangan ketawa,” kata Ojan. Suaranya bergetar. “Lututku sakit.” Dimas cepat-cepat berlari memanggil Bu Guru.

“Please don’t laugh,” said Ojan. His voice was shaky. “My knee hurts.” Dimas ran quickly to get the teacher.

4Halaman empatPage four

Vivi berhenti tertawa. Ia melihat lutut Ojan yang merah. “Maaf, Ojan,” kata Vivi pelan. “Aku kira kamu jatuhnya lucu.”

Vivi stopped laughing. She saw Ojan’s red knee. “I’m sorry, Ojan,” Vivi said softly. “I thought your fall looked funny.”

5Halaman limaPage five

Bu Guru datang bersama Dimas. Bu Guru membersihkan luka Ojan dengan air. Byur, pelan-pelan. “Jatuh itu bagian dari belajar,” kata Bu Guru. “Mau coba lagi hari ini, atau besok?”

The teacher came with Dimas. She cleaned Ojan’s cut with water. Splish, gently. “Falling is part of learning,” said the teacher. “Do you want to try again today, or tomorrow?”

6Halaman enamPage six

Ojan berpikir. Ia melihat sepedanya. Ia melihat jalan taman. “Sekarang,” kata Ojan. “Tapi pelan-pelan.”

Ojan thought about it. He looked at his bike. He looked at the park path. “Now,” said Ojan. “But slowly.”

7Halaman tujuhPage seven

Vivi berdiri di ujung jalan. “Ayo, Ojan! Ke sini!” Ojan mengayuh. Dimas ikut menghitung: satu, dua, tiga kayuhan. Belum jatuh!

Vivi stood at the end of the path. “Come on, Ojan! Over here!” Ojan pedaled. Dimas counted along: one, two, three pushes. Still riding!

8Halaman delapanPage eight

Di ujung jalan, Vivi bertepuk tangan. Prok, prok, prok! Kali ini Vivi tidak tertawa. Kali ini Vivi bersorak. “Hore! Ojan bisa!” Dimas juga ikut bersorak.

At the end of the path, Vivi clapped her hands. Clap, clap, clap! This time, Vivi did not laugh. This time, Vivi cheered. “Hooray! Ojan did it!” Dimas cheered too.

Pesan moral · Moral of the story

Kalau teman jatuh, jangan ditertawakan. Bantu dan semangati dia.

When a friend falls, don’t laugh. Help them and cheer them on.

Nilai yang termuat dalam ceritaValues in this story

1. Perbuatan terpuji atau baik · Good deeds

Menyemangati teman yang sedang belajar, bukan menertawakannya.

Cheering on a friend who is learning, instead of laughing at them.

2. Perbuatan tidak terpuji atau jangan ditiru · Don’t copy this

Menertawakan teman yang jatuh atau sedang kesulitan.

Laughing at a friend who falls or is having a hard time.

3. Hak Asasi Manusia · Human rights

Hak setiap orang untuk diperlakukan dengan hormat dan tidak direndahkan.

Everyone has the right to be treated with respect and not be put down.

4. Ajak Bicara (Orang tua/keluarga/Guru) · Let’s talk (parents, family, teachers)

Apa yang bisa kamu katakan kalau temanmu jatuh?

What can you say when your friend falls?

“Kamu tidak apa-apa?”“Are you okay?” “Ayo, aku bantu.”“Come on, I’ll help you.” “Coba lagi, kamu pasti bisa!”“Try again, you can do it!”
